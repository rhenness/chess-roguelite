# Main-menu background

The abandoned chess hall appears only on the main menu. The fixed background
fills the viewport behind the header and menu, with a soft dark overlay. The
portrait companion keeps torchlit columns visible on phones. Menu
controls use opaque green surfaces and ivory text to remain readable.

Final asset: [public/main-menu-chess-hall.jpg](../public/main-menu-chess-hall.jpg).
Portrait asset: [public/main-menu-chess-hall-mobile.jpg](../public/main-menu-chess-hall-mobile.jpg).
Generated with the built-in image generation tool and encoded as a JPEG at
quality 90 with original dimensions (1536 × 1024 landscape; 1024 × 1536 portrait).
The original generated PNGs are retained in Codex's generated-images directory.

## Generation prompt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: full-page main-menu background artwork for Knightfall, a chess roguelite.
Primary request: a beautiful abandoned chess hall inside a medieval castle, with a worn ivory-and-deep-green checkerboard stone floor receding into the distance and massive rook-shaped stone columns along the edges.
Style/medium: premium stylized fantasy game environment painting, softly rendered tactile stone, restrained cinematic lighting, elegant rather than grim or frightening.
Composition/framing: wide landscape 3:2 composition, eye-level view down the hall, slightly elevated enough to see the chessboard floor in the lower third. The middle 40 percent must stay spacious, quiet and dark forest green for real menu controls to be added later. Keep the far center atmospheric with soft green haze rather than a prominent doorway. Columns along both sides resemble castle rooks with subtle crenellated capitals; place a few warm golden torches on columns near the outer edges, and some smaller distant torches just inside the central third so a narrow centered phone crop still retains torchlight and a sense of architecture. Broad uninterrupted calm space in the upper center for a logo and title. Checkerboard floor visible across the bottom, including the center crop.
Lighting/mood: inviting adventure and quiet mystery, warm amber torchlight against deep emerald shadows, gentle haze, soft detail and no harsh highlights in the central UI area.
Color palette: deep green #142b25 and forest green #234b40, aged ivory #e9e3d4, antique gold #dfbc77.
Constraints: background environment only; no words, no lettering, no logo, no watermark, no UI, no borders, no decorative frame, no foreground knight or chess pieces, no characters, no central staircase, no bright central focal point. Fill the entire image with the environment; opaque background.
```

## Portrait generation prompt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: portrait mobile background for the Knightfall chess roguelite main menu.
Input image 1: style and environment reference, the approved abandoned chess hall. Create a portrait companion composition of this same hall.
Primary request: render the same atmospheric abandoned medieval chess hall in a tall portrait 2:3 image, 1024 by 1536. Retain deep green stone, subtle aged gold heraldic banners, warm torchlight, worn ivory-and-green checkerboard stone floor, and softly painted premium fantasy game artwork.
Composition: keep the central 45 percent quiet deep green shadow and haze for real menu controls added later. Place slender rook-shaped stone columns around 20 percent and 80 percent of image width, so a narrow phone crop still clearly shows them. Include warm lit torches on these columns around the upper-middle of the composition, outside the calm center. Side architecture should remain subdued. A receding checkerboard floor begins near the lower third and extends to the bottom. Broad calm dark green space in the upper center for the real logo and title. No giant foreground columns that consume the center, no bright central light. Perspective and materials match the reference; recompose the environment for portrait use instead of cropping the landscape.
Constraints: background environment only, image fills entire canvas, opaque; no words, text, logo, UI, watermark, borders, decorative frames, foreground chess pieces or characters. Preserve the green, ivory and antique gold palette, softly detailed stone, and inviting mysterious mood.
```
