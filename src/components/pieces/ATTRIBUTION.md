# Custom chess pieces

The Obsidian Order and Gilded Court SVGs adapt the silhouettes of Cburnett's chess pieces,
as distributed in react-chessboard's default set:
https://github.com/Clariity/react-chessboard/blob/main/src/pieces.tsx

Original artwork: Cburnett, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/

These adaptations retain that license. Obsidian Order changes include angular
outlines, faceted stone shading, silver and charcoal palettes, and crimson gem
inlays. Gilded Court changes include ivory and dark green enamel shading,
gold outlines and bases, ornamental engraving, and emerald inlays.
